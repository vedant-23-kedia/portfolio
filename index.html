About

the short version

Most AI demos die the moment a real user does something unexpected. I'm interested in the part after that.

I started writing C in a university lab and ended up building platforms that talk to actual customers. The through-line hasn't changed: work out what someone really needs, then make a machine do it reliably at 3am without anyone watching.

My work sits where AI meets plumbing. A Gemini-backed engagement platform for a startup client. A guard layer that inspects LLM requests before they reach the model. A tracker that reads a Gmail inbox and keeps a spreadsheet honest. Less model research, more getting models to behave in production.

Two internships taught me the unglamorous half — eliciting requirements from stakeholders at Planstalk, and executing test cases against Spatial AI and AR builds at Multiset AI. I write the spec, build it, then try to break it.
